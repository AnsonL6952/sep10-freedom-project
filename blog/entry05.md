## Explanation of Aframe
Aframe is a complex tool where it shows 3d models in which you can apply meshes, shapes, textures to a given 3d world.

    You can apply Aframe to a variety of technologies on your device and media such as

 * your project
 *  a game
 * virtual reality.

##### Positioning of Shapes and variables
 ```(3,#,#) positioned the shape or entity left of right```

``` (#,3,#) positioned the shape entity up or down ```

```(#,#,3) positioned the shape or entity in or out```
